&#x09;								**Decisions**



**ADR-001 — The Atomic Object**



**Decision:**

**The Collection is the atomic unit of the ApingX platform.**



**Every meaningful object—products, credentials, contributors, stories, rewards and provenance—belongs to exactly one Collection.**



**Reason:**

**ApingX publishes finite collections. Organising the platform around Collections reflects both the business model and the cultural philosophy of the brand.**



**Consequence:**

**All future architecture will be collection-centric rather than product-centric.**



**ADR-002 – Collections Move Forward Through Time**



**Decision:**

**Collections progress through a one-way lifecycle from idea to legacy. Once published, the Collection Charter, edition size and economic rights become immutable. Every significant action generates an event that becomes part of the permanent provenance timeline.**



**Reason:**

**ApingX is a publishing platform. Publishing creates history. History should be additive, never rewritten.**
