&#x09;						**Glossary**



**Archive**



The permanent historical record of every collection published by ApingX.



The Archive preserves each collection's story, contributors, provenance and legacy.



Every collection becomes part of the Archive.



**Collection**



A limited-edition body of work published by ApingX.



A collection may include multiple garments and accessories released under a single creative vision.



Example:



Collection 001 – Provenance



**Publish**



The official release of a collection.



ApingX does not "drop" collections.



We publish them.



**Provenance**



The complete history of a collection.



Including:



creators

contributors

publication date

edition size

ownership history

supporting media

archival information



Fashion deserves provenance.



Founder



The individual or organisation responsible for initiating a collection.



The Founder represents the beginning of the collection's story.



Contributor



Anyone who materially contributes to the creation of a collection.



Examples include:



artists

designers

photographers

musicians

developers

collaborators

Owner



The current custodian of a Contributor Credential.



Ownership represents participation in the history of a collection.



Contributor Credential



A digital certificate recognising a contributor's role in a collection.



Internally this may be implemented using NFTs.



Externally we rarely use the term NFT.



Collection Certificate



The digital certificate associated with a collection.



It permanently records participation and provenance.



Reward



A distribution made to recognised contributors according to ApingX's business rules.



Rewards recognise contribution.



They are not guaranteed investment returns.



Collection Revenue



Revenue generated through the sale of products belonging to a specific collection.



Allocation



The percentage of collection revenue assigned to eligible contributors.



Allocations are business rules managed by ApingX.



Archive Record



The permanent record created when a collection is published.



Contains:



Story

Contributors

Creative assets

Timeline

Edition size

Provenance

Story



The narrative behind a collection.



Every collection must have one.



Edition Size



The total number of physical items produced within a collection.



Once published, edition size should remain permanent.



Scarcity creates value.



Genesis Collection



The first collection published by ApingX.



The beginning of the Archive.



(We may revisit whether this remains an internal name or not.)



Provenance Score (Future)



A potential measure of how complete a collection's historical record is.



This is only an idea for now.



House



ApingX itself.



A creative organisation dedicated to publishing fashion collections.



House Principles



The values defined by the Constitution.



Every decision should strengthen these principles.



Council



The collection of specialist AI roles responsible for reviewing engineering decisions.



Examples:



CTO

Founder

Finance

Security

Solana

Frontend

Constitution



The governing principles of ApingX.



If uncertainty exists, the Constitution takes precedence.



Manifesto



The public expression of why ApingX exists.



It inspires.



The Constitution governs.



Playbook



The internal operating guide explaining how ApingX builds products, makes decisions and develops software.



AOS (ApingX Operating System)



The collection of principles, documents and processes that define how ApingX thinks and operates.
