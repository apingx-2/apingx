&#x09;						**Values**



.

1\. Provenance

Everything deserves a story.

We believe fashion should not disappear after checkout. Every collection deserves a permanent history.



2\. Craft

Quality before quantity.

Whether it's a garment, photograph, illustration or line of code, we strive to create things worth remembering.



3\. Community

Great collections are never created alone.

Every contributor matters.

Whether you're an artist, founder, photographer, developer or supporter, your contribution deserves recognition.



4\. Curiosity

Question everything.

Fashion evolves.

Technology evolves.

We evolve.

We are never too proud to improve.

(I particularly like this one because it reflects the way you've approached our conversations.)



5\. Authenticity

Never fake culture.

We create because we love fashion.

Not because something is trending.



6\. Legacy

Build things that outlive us.

We don't chase hype.

We build collections people will remember years from now.
