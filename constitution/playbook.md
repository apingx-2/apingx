&#x09;						**Playbook**



**Vision**



ApingX exists to ensure that fashion is remembered not just worn.



**Mission**



To build the world's most trusted archive of limited-edition fashion collections, where every collection has a permanent story, every contributor receives recognition, and every owner becomes part of its provenance.



**Manifesto**



*Fashion deserves provenance.*



We don't believe fashion should disappear after checkout.

Every collection deserves a permanent history.

Every creator deserves recognition.

Every supporter deserves to become part of the story.

We don't sell clothing.

We publish collections

We are the House of Provenance



**Golden Rule**



The blockchain records ownership. The database records business logic.



**Never List**



* Build features just because they're trendy.
* Sacrifice the fashion brand for crypto hype.
* Make customers learn blockchain terminology.
* Reward speculation over genuine participation.
* Compromise the quality of our clothing to fund Web3 features.
* Release a collection that doesn't have a story.
* Make technology more visible than the fashion.



**Brand Principles**



* Fashion comes first.
* Community over speculation.
* Scarcity creates value.
* Every collection tells a story.
* NFTs represent contributors.
* Blockchain proves ownership.
* Database runs the business.
* Technology should disappear into the background.
* Every design decision must strengthen the brand.
* Build for the next decade, not the next bull market.
