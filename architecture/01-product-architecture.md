**\*\*Status:\*\* Approved**



**\*\*Owner:\*\* Founder \& CTO**



**---**



**# Purpose**



**This document defines the core business objects of the ApingX platform.**



**It intentionally avoids implementation details.**



**It answers one question:**



**\*\*"What are we building?"\*\***



**Technology exists to support these definitions—not define them.**



**---**



**# Product Vision**



**ApingX is a crypto-native fashion house that publishes limited-edition collections with permanent provenance.**



**Born from crypto culture, ApingX creates collectible fashion inspired by the ideas, humour, philosophy and history of the digital asset ecosystem.**



**Every published collection becomes a permanent cultural artefact comprising physical products, creative works, recognised contributors and immutable historical records.**



**Blockchain is used to authenticate provenance and preserve trust, while remaining largely invisible to the customer experience.**



**---**



**# The Atomic Unit**



**The atomic unit of the ApingX platform is the \*\*Collection\*\*.**



**Everything belongs to exactly one Collection.**



**Examples include:**



**- Products**

**- Credentials**

**- Contributors**

**- Story**

**- Reward Allocations**

**- Orders**

**- Archive**



**---**



**# Core Objects**



**## Collection**



**A Collection is a published body of creative work.**



**Every Collection contains:**



**- Story**

**- Products**

**- Contributors**

**- Credentials**

**- Collection Charter**

**- Provenance**



**Rules:**



**- Every Collection has one Collection Charter.**

**- Every Collection has at least one Product.**

**- Every Collection has at least one Credential.**

**- Collections become immutable once published.**



**---**



**## Product**



**A Product is a physical item belonging to a Collection.**



**Examples:**



**- T-Shirt**

**- Hoodie**

**- Cap**

**- Socks**



**Rules:**



**- Products belong to one Collection only.**

**- Products cannot move between Collections.**

**- Product quantities are fixed upon publication.**



**Future versions may support individual product identities for provenance.**



**---**



**## Credential**



**A Credential records a permanent relationship with a Collection.**



**Credential types may include:**



**- Founder**

**- Artist**

**- Photographer**

**- Patron**

**- Community**

**- Charity**



**Some Credentials include immutable economic rights.**



**Others provide recognition only.**



**Rules:**



**- Credentials belong to one Collection.**

**- Economic allocations become immutable after publication.**

**- Ownership may transfer depending on Credential type.**



**---**



**## Contributor**



**A Contributor is an individual or organisation recognised within a Collection.**



**Examples:**



**- Founder**

**- Artist**

**- Designer**

**- Photographer**

**- Musician**

**- Manufacturer**

**- Developer**



**Recognition is permanent.**



**---**



**## Collection Charter**



**The Collection Charter is the constitutional record of the Collection.**



**It defines:**



**- Edition Size**

**- Products**

**- Contributors**

**- Credential Allocations**

**- Publication Date**



**Once published, the Charter cannot be altered.**



**---**



**# Product Philosophy**



**Every Collection should satisfy five principles.**



**## 1. Story**



**Every Collection tells a story.**



**---**



**## 2. Scarcity**



**Edition sizes are fixed.**



**No additional units will ever be produced.**



**---**



**## 3. Recognition**



**Contributors receive permanent recognition.**



**---**



**## 4. Provenance**



**Every important event is preserved.**



**History should never be rewritten.**



**---**



**## 5. Trust**



**Immutable economic rights create confidence between ApingX and its community.**

**↓**



**Review**

**Active**

**Sold Out**

**Legacy**

**---**



**# Design Principles**



**When building ApingX:**



**- Collections come before Products.**

**- Story comes before technology.**

**- Provenance is permanent.**

**- Blockchain supports trust.**

**- Simplicity beats complexity.**

**- Crypto is our cultural origin—not a barrier to entry.**



**---**



**# Version One Scope**



**Version One includes:**



**- Collection publishing**

**- Limited products**

**- Credentials**

**- Reward allocations**

**- Contributor recognition**

**- Provenance**

**- Collection archive**



**The following are explicitly outside Version One:**



**- DAO governance**

**- Community voting**

**- Fractional ownership**

**- Secondary marketplace**

**- Mobile application**



**---**



**# Success Criteria**



**Version One succeeds if a Collection can be:**



**1. Created**

**2. Published**

**3. Sold**

**4. Archived**

**5. Remembered**



**without compromising scarcity, provenance or trust.**
