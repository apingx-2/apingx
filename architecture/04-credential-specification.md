**# Credential Specification**



**## Purpose**



**A Credential is a traditional Solana NFT (Metaplex Digital Asset) that permanently represents participation in an ApingX Collection.**



**Some Credentials carry immutable revenue sharing rights.**



**Others exist solely for recognition.**



**The Credential is the permanent proof of participation.**



**---**



**## Core Principles**



**- One Credential belongs to one Collection.**

**- A Credential can never move between Collections.**

**- Reward allocation is immutable.**

**- Ownership is transferable.**

**- History is permanent.**



**---**



**## Credential Types**



**Version One supports:**



**- Founder**

**- Contributor**



**Future versions may include:**



**- Patron**

**- Charity**

**- Community**

**- Museum**



**---**



**## On-chain Data**



**Stored permanently on Solana.**



**- Collection ID**

**- Credential ID**

**- Credential Type**

**- Reward Allocation %**

**- Collection Number**

**- Mint Address**

**- Issue Timestamp**



**These values can never change after publication.**



**---**



**## Off-chain Data**



**Stored in PostgreSQL.**



**- Collection Name**

**- Collection Story**

**- Images**

**- Contributor Biography**

**- Social Links**

**- Display Metadata**

**- Reward History**



**These can evolve over time.**



**---**



**## Transfer Rules**



**Credentials may be transferred freely.**



**Ownership is determined solely by the current wallet holder.**



**ApingX distributes revenue to the current owner.**



**---**



**## Marketplace Compatibility**



**Credentials must remain compatible with:**



**- Magic Eden**

**- Tensor**

**- Other major Solana NFT marketplaces**



**ApingX does not provide marketplace functionality.**



**---**



**## Reward Allocation**



**Reward Allocation is immutable.**



**Example**



**Credential #001**



**Founder**



**Allocation**



**10%**



**Forever.**



**Ownership may change.**



**Allocation never changes.**



**---**



**## Metadata**



**Each Credential should display:**



**Collection**



**Credential Type**



**Allocation**



**Issue Date**



**Edition**



**Collection Image**



**Description**



**---**



**## Design Goal**



**The Credential should feel less like a speculative NFT and more like a permanent certificate of participation within the history of an ApingX Collection.**
